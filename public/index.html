Etherfi Collector is a private financial data monitoring and Google Sheets collection tool. It helps its owner keep a dated record of supported onchain borrowing, risk and vault observations for review.

Read the privacy policy →

Purpose and access

The separate collector implementation reads supported public blockchain contracts. When configured and explicitly authorized by its owner, it reads and writes designated areas of a Google Sheets workbook to preserve observations, source details and collection checkpoints.

Google Sheets access supports recordkeeping and verification of saved data. The tool does not execute asset transfers, trades, borrowing, repayments or wallet approvals.

This website provides application and privacy information for Google OAuth configuration. It has no sign-in, account connection, financial dashboard or data-entry form. Visiting it does not authorize Google access or activate the collector.

Controlled by the owner

Google access requires a separate consent step. The owner controls the destination workbook, deployment and credentials, and can revoke access or stop collection. The privacy policy explains the data flow, storage and limits.

An independent personal project. Not operated by or affiliated with ether.fi, Google or Render.
